## Problem -> solution -> value -> outcome (time and money) -> scaling
Priya had 37 names in a spreadsheet. Who is waiting? Whose trial ends Friday? Who quietly stopped coming?

Sorting it out took about 40 minutes a week, and a missed trial can mean a lost learner.

In skillBNK, learners sit in four lists (Pending, Trial, Approved, Inactive), and the Last Active column shows who has been away.

Example: 40 minutes becomes about 5. That is roughly 29 hours back a year. (Example calculation, not a measured result.)

Whether it is 37 learners or 370, it is the same four lists.

Full walkthrough on YouTube: https://youtu.be/cTX63O9qP7I
